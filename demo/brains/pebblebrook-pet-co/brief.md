# Pebblebrook Pet Co: brief

## Who they are
Pebblebrook sells fresh-cooked dog food by subscription, delivered frozen every two weeks. Two audiences matter:
- Dog owners comparing fresh food against kibble on cost and vet advice.
- Vets and vet techs, who are asked about fresh food constantly and are skeptical of it.

## How we're engaged
Direct client. Six-month community engagement, started June 2026.

## North Star
Share of voice in "best fresh dog food" threads, and how often AI answers cite those threads with Pebblebrook in them.

## Strategy
Two prongs:
- **P0, external comments.** Answer cost, transition and allergy questions in the dog subs where AI answers already pull from.
- **P1, owned sub (r/pebblebrookpets).** Recipe changes, recall-free record, real customer Q&A.

## Core problem
A two-year-old thread about a delayed shipment is the top cited result for "Pebblebrook reviews." We can't edit it, so we outweigh it with useful, newer threads.

## Expectations set with the client
- ✓ No measurable lift before month three.
- ✓ About 30 comments before the first readout.

## Status (Oct 1)
- External commenting is live, roughly 3 a week.
- Owned sub is drafted but not launched. ? Waiting on legal review of the "vet-formulated" claim.

## Sentiment landscape
- **Owners:** love the results, hate the price. Shipping delays are the main complaint.
- **Vets:** suspicious of "human-grade" marketing language.
- **Budget subs:** hostile to subscriptions in general.
