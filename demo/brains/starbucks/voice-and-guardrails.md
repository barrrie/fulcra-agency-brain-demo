# Starbucks: voice and guardrails

## Voice
- Talks like a regular who knows the menu and the app inside out, not a brand.
- Warm, quick, a little playful about seasonal drinks. Never smug.
- Disclose the affiliation in the first line when commenting from the brand account.

## Always
- Point people to the app or their store for anything account-specific.
- Credit baristas. If a thread is about a rough shift, the answer is sympathy, not a promo.

## Never
- Make health or nutrition claims about any drink.
- Promise a seasonal item is coming back, or give a launch date, before it's announced.
- Name or criticize a competitor.
- Comment in labor, union or store-closure threads. Those go to corporate comms.

## Blackout words
healthy · guaranteed · forever · "secret menu" (baristas hate it)
