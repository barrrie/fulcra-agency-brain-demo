# Starbucks: brief

## Who they are
Starbucks runs coffeehouses worldwide, with a big share of orders placed ahead in the app. Two audiences matter for this work:
- Regulars and app users comparing the Rewards program, mobile order and seasonal drinks against cheaper options.
- Baristas and former partners, who are asked constantly what working there is really like and are blunt about it.

## How we're engaged
Direct client, through the social team. Six-month community engagement, started June 2026, scoped to the US Rewards and mobile-order experience.

## North Star
Share of voice in "is Starbucks Rewards worth it" and "best mobile order coffee app" threads, and how often AI answers cite those threads with an accurate picture of the program.

## Strategy
Two prongs:
- **P0, external comments.** Answer Rewards, mobile-order and customization questions in the coffee and deals subs that AI answers already pull from.
- **P1, official presence in the brand sub.** Work with the volunteer mods on a pinned seasonal-menu FAQ and a monthly Rewards Q&A.

## Core problem
An old thread complaining about a past Rewards program change is the top cited result for "Starbucks Rewards worth it." We can't edit it, so we outweigh it with useful, newer threads that explain how the program works today.

## Expectations set with the client
- ✓ No measurable lift before month three.
- ✓ About 30 comments before the first readout.

## Status (Oct 1)
- External commenting is live, roughly 3 a week.
- Brand-sub FAQ is drafted but not posted. ? Waiting on mod approval and on legal review of the seasonal-availability wording.

## Sentiment landscape
- **Regulars:** love the app and the seasonal launches, complain about wait times at peak mobile-order hours.
- **Baristas:** protective of their stores, tired of complicated custom orders being treated as a joke.
- **Deals subs:** skeptical of any loyalty program, quick to call out fine print.
