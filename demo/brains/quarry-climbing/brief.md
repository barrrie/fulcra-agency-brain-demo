# Quarry Climbing: brief

## Who they are
Quarry runs nine climbing gyms across three cities. Members are a mix of committed climbers and people who tried it once on a date.

## How we're engaged
Direct client. Three-month pilot, started September 2026, focused on the two newest gyms.

## North Star

## Strategy
