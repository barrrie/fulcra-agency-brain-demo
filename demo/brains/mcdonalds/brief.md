# McDonald's: brief

## Who they are
McDonald's is a global quick-service restaurant chain, mostly run by local franchise owner-operators. Customers range from daily breakfast regulars to people who only open the app when there's a deal.

## How we're engaged
Through a regional co-op of owner-operators. Three-month pilot, started September 2026, focused on two test markets.

## North Star
New app sign-ups in the two test markets where "Reddit" is picked in the post-signup "how did you hear about us" question.

## Strategy
- **P0, local subs.** Be a helpful presence in each city's subreddit when people ask about late-night food, road-trip stops or what's new in town.
- **P1, r/McDonalds.** Behind-the-counter posts from a local owner-operator.

## Core problem
Locals rally around the independent burger spots. McDonald's reads as "the chain," even though the restaurants are owned by people who live there.

## Expectations set with the client
- ✓ Pilot is judged on sign-up attribution, not upvotes.
