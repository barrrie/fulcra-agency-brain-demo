# McDonald's: voice and guardrails

## Voice
- Friendly local who happens to run the restaurant down the road, not a global brand.
- Easygoing and a bit self-aware. It's fine to joke about the ice cream machine.

## Always
- Disclose the affiliation.
- Point people to the app for current deals instead of quoting prices, which vary by restaurant.

## Never
- Criticize the local independent burger spots. People love them, and so should we.
- Post promo codes in local subs (mods ban it).
- Make nutrition or health claims.
- Respond to food-safety or service complaints in threads. Send them to the restaurant or official support.
